# Code of conduct

Silt is built by people who disagree about code often, and that's fine. What isn't fine is making anyone feel unwelcome while doing it.

## What we expect
- Be kind and patient, with newcomers most of all. Assume good faith.
- Criticise code and ideas, not people. Say what's wrong and, where you can, what would be better.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on a change, even when you disagree with it. Reopening it with new information is welcome; repeating the same argument isn't.
- Respect people's time: keep issues and pull requests on their subject.

## What isn't acceptable
- Harassment of any kind, in public or private: insults, slurs, unwelcome sexual attention, threats, or deliberately following someone around the project.
- Discriminating against anyone for who they are: their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Anything else that a reasonable person would see as inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where it applies
In every space the project has: its repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and anywhere someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement
Report behaviour that breaks this code to the project's maintainers, privately. Reports are kept confidential.

The maintainers decide what happens: a private word, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban from the project's spaces, depending on what happened and whether it keeps happening. They remove comments, commits, code and issues that break this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
